# Brag Plan: FLOW

## What is this app?
FLOW is a local console and controller that runs real delivery work through several AI coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, Copilot) under one governed lifecycle — nothing crosses a boundary, and no agent writes a line of code, without a human typing the exact words to allow it.

## The angle
Every other agent tool answers "what changed." FLOW is built to answer the harder question a CTO actually asks: what was it allowed to read, whose authority did it act on, and what would you show a client who asked. The video's premise: this is the one governance layer that treats "read-only" as a claim that has to be proven, not a checkbox — and it proves it on screen, in the product's own UI, not in marketing copy.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A blinking cursor on a plain terminal-style line: `Type exactly:` — then the phrase `APPROVE PLAN 20260918-...` types itself out character by character, key-tick by key-tick, and stops. Nothing happens yet. The tension of the video is: nothing happens until this line is typed.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The typed phrase completes and the screen unlocks into the routing/execution matrix — four columns arriving in sequence (account → method → model & effort → cost & ceiling), each landing with its own small tick, because the product's own design principle is that these are four different things and are never collapsed into one badge.
- A connector row flips to a red "blocked" state with its real reason text on screen ("no role in this run holds this connector" / "not scoped, safe subset unproven") — dramatizing the product's actual rule: unproven read-only is reported blocked, never marked safe by default.
- A one-line payoff card: `no --yolo. no danger-full-access. fde doctor fails if one ever appears.` — delivered flat, like a spec line, because the line is already funny/impressive without embellishment.

## Outro / punchline
Hold on the thesis line, full width, serif display type on the product's own warm paper background: **"Read-only is enforced, or it is not claimed."** Then a quiet cut to the wordmark **FLOW** and one small line beneath it: the controller command, `fde-start`.

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result, taken directly from this run's own lifecycle:
1. **Entry** — a request lands (a one-line ask, plain text).
2. **Key action** — the combined plan-and-role summary appears, and the operator types `APPROVE PLAN <run-id>` — the single gate that unlocks everything downstream.
3. **Result** — the frozen execution matrix lights up: the right account, at the right model and effort, under a cost ceiling, doing exactly the approved slice — no more, no less.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a quiet, premium product film for infrastructure/governance tooling — the kind of restraint that reads as competence to both a CTO and the engineer who has to defend it later.
- Interpretation: fewer, longer-held scenes; confident pacing over frantic cuts; typography carries the story more than motion; nothing plays for a laugh — the impressive claims are stated flatly and left alone.

## Share copy (draft)

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, restrained corporate bed — confidence, not hype
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (114.84 BPM), used at a quiet, low posture rather than its natural upbeat energy — treat it as a bed, not a driver
- Music treatment: enters under the hook at low volume, small volume lift under the matrix reveal, settles back down for the outro hold, clean fade out on the final wordmark
- Music cue guidance: preset read from `cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.md`. Target strong cues near 3.70s (hook → reveal cut) and 8.44s (reveal → blocked-connector beat); let the outro hold ride under the tail without chasing further cues.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle only — the matrix columns' arrival ticks may sit slightly on-beat; no waveform-driven glow or bars
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, professional restraint — a key-tick per typed character in the hook, one small "confirm" tick when the phrase completes, a soft tick per matrix column landing, a single low tone on the blocked-row flip
- Audio-coupled moments: the typed `APPROVE PLAN` phrase (key ticks), the four-column matrix reveal (sequential ticks), the connector flipping to blocked (one low/negative tone)
- Restraint rule: no music swell or triumphant sting anywhere — the product's whole claim is restraint and proof, not excitement

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The gate — 3s
Plain terminal-style line on the paper background, mono type: `Type exactly:` then `APPROVE PLAN 20260918-...` typing out character by character. Cursor blinks and stops on the completed phrase. Nothing else on screen.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the phrase types out character by character, key-tick per character, ending on a held, completed line.
Audio intent: quiet tension — the sound of something about to unlock, not yet unlocked.
Audio-coupled idea: key-tick per typed character; one slightly heavier "confirm" tick the instant the phrase completes.
Music: bed enters low under this scene.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The matrix unlocks — 6s
The screen opens into the real routing/execution matrix: four columns arrive in sequence — account, method, model & effort, cost & ceiling — each with a short settle (not a flash). Display-serif label above: "Four different things. Never one badge."
Sequential/interaction: yes — four columns land one at a time, left to right, each pausing to be read before the next arrives.
Audio intent: quiet confidence, mechanism clicking into place.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft tick per column landing, evenly spaced, not beat-crammed.
Music: small volume lift as the matrix completes.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Blocked, and it says why — 5s
A connector row (real product copy) flips from pending to a red "blocked" badge, with its actual reason text settling beside it — e.g. "no role in this run holds this connector." Held long enough to read in full.
Sequential/interaction: none — one clean state flip, held.
Audio intent: a small, dry, negative tone — not alarming, just honest.
Audio-coupled idea: single low tick synced to the badge flip.
Music: settles back to its quiet bed level.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The claim, then the mark — 6s
Full-width serif display line on paper background: "Read-only is enforced, or it is not claimed." Holds. Quiet cut to the wordmark FLOW, small mono line beneath: `fde-start`.
Sequential/interaction: none — two held cards, no motion for its own sake.
Audio intent: settle and land — the last word, not a flourish.
Audio-coupled idea: none.
Music: fades cleanly under the wordmark, ending with the video.
Transition mood: soft crossfade between the two halves of the scene, hard stop at black/paper.

**Music mood for this video:** restrained corporate/cinematic bed, treated quietly throughout
**Audio summary:** a low, confident bed that never swells, punctuated only by small mechanical ticks that mirror exactly what's happening on screen — typing, arriving, flipping, then silence.
